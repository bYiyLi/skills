# Channel work and learning

Read for the requested channel operation. Apply the root Skill's authority and
live-source rules; the actions below do not independently authorize writes.

## Use evidence that changes operating decisions

Connect real events and user questions to content/community participation, user
behavior, qualified intent, activation, and new questions. Turn observations into
hypotheses and experiments before promoting experience into Playbooks or the
cross-channel methodology.

Qualified intent includes questions about connecting local projects, installation,
pricing, trials and actual use. Views, likes, saves and follows diagnose reach;
they do not substitute for intent. Prefer real product behavior, screenshots,
terminal output, diffs and user language over invented marketing proof.
Recurring work that produces neither user results nor decision-changing evidence
needs redesign or a proposed stop; changing its schedule still requires authority.

## Research and preparation

Use the logged-in platform when current rankings, reactions, comments or account
state are the subject. Public web search can supplement that evidence. For each
useful sample, capture the topic/title, channel, observed interaction snapshot,
format, hook, comment demand and relevance to Nexum. Under recording authority,
store samples in `样本库`; reusable inferences start in `运营经验` as `观察` or `假设`.
An external sample alone does not establish a Nexum rule.

Choose content from real development events, demand, experiments and applicable
experience. Give an item a primary job such as discovery, utility, proof, trust,
interaction or conversion rather than maximizing all at once. Record authorized
work in `内容池`. Use only experience whose channel, evidence and status apply;
`待验证` may guide an experiment, not a claimed formula. Adapt copy, pacing, proof,
CTA and conversion path to each channel instead of mechanically cross-posting.

## Community participation

Evaluate relevant discussions in `社区机会` under recording authority. Prefer
material user overlap and a contribution supported by Nexum experience, data or
technical context. A comment should help even after every Nexum mention is removed.
Do not participate solely for visibility, use mechanical profile/DM diversions,
or target a competitor post's expressed purchase intent for poaching. Useful
technical discussion is allowed. Record actual participation, observable response,
qualified leads and learning without inventing reactions.

## Publish, communicate and inspect

Check the current Playbook, target account, prepared content, evidence and action
authority before publishing, editing, commenting, replying or sending a DM. Honor
any required immediate confirmation through the browser/runtime or other tool.
Never make misleading claims, fake engagement, spam or disallowed outbound contact.

Re-inspect the resulting platform state after the action. A click or attempted
command is not a sent/published result. Update the matching Feishu record and
required notification/log through the root's logging route. If the external action
succeeded but recording failed, repair recording only and report partial completion.

## Monitoring, leads and review

Update observable metrics on the existing content record rather than duplicating
records per measurement window. The 2h/24h/72h windows are diagnostic options when
applicable, not mandatory jobs or permission to invent unavailable metrics.
Preserve original qualified questions in `用户线索`; generic praise is not a lead.

A review explains what changed, what the evidence supports, remaining uncertainty
and the next decision. Separate correlation from causation. A read-only review
reports this without modifying operating records.

## Experiments and promotion

Use `运营实验` for a belief with a defined variable and success criterion. Do not
retroactively call an ordinary post a controlled experiment. In `运营经验`, retain
supporting observations, counterexamples and applicability; conflicting evidence
triggers revalidation, and one successful post does not establish a rule.

A channel Playbook candidate needs repeated Nexum-specific support with no
unresolved material contradiction in its claimed scope. A methodology candidate
also needs cross-channel usefulness and repeated Nexum support or an explicit
operating-governance decision; platform mechanics remain in channel Playbooks.

Under update authority, promotion changes the corresponding Playbook or
`Nexum 运营资料库 / 运营方法` and records its location in the experience. Invalidating
or replacing a rule updates both places so stale guidance is not reused.
Dynamic tactics belong in live Feishu sources. Change this Skill only when its
stable execution protocol changes and Skill modification is authorized.
